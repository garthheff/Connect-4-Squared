Your turn
Meta grid has gravity. Only bottom row is playable at first. Win a mini board to claim the meta tile and unlock the tile above it. Draw resets the mini board.

How to play Connect 4²

Win small boards to build a winning line on the big board.

  1. Choose a playable mini-board. Only the bottom row of the big board is available at the start.
  2. Drop a disc. Click a column. Your disc falls to the lowest empty slot. Red moves first; Yellow moves second.
  3. Claim a mini-board. Connect four discs of your colour horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. The board becomes your coloured tile and is locked.
  4. Unlock the board above. A claimed tile unlocks the mini-board directly above it, whichever player claimed it.
  5. Win the big board. Connect four claimed tiles of your colour horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

Switching boards

On each turn you can use any playable mini-board. You do not have to follow your opponent. Close the mini-board to choose another, or select Previous board to return to where you last played if it is still available.

Draws and highlights

A mini-board that fills without a winner resets to empty and stays playable. A white ring marks the latest disc; green outlines show winning lines. Click claimed boards to inspect their winning discs. They cannot receive further moves.

Game modes

Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard to play the computer. Changing difficulty starts a fresh game.

For two devices, select Online friend. The host shares an invite link, the guest returns a reply code, and the host accepts it. The host is Red and controls new games. Keep both pages open. Some networks cannot connect without a relay.

Changing themes keeps your game. New game clears all boards.

Play a friend online

Create an invite link and send it to your friend. They open it and send their reply code back. Paste that reply here to connect.

Direct connection: some networks may require a relay, which this mode does not use.
